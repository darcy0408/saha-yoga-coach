# Contest submission draft

## Saha: move with awareness

Home practice is convenient but generic video cannot respond when someone needs a clearer view, a gentler variation, or a slower progression. Saha is a privacy-first Java 26 desktop yoga coach that converts local anonymous body landmarks into small, supportive suggestions and explainable routine changes.

Java is the product core: JavaFX presents onboarding through progress; immutable records carry landmarks, poses, and derived sessions; sealed outcomes ensure uncertain frames cannot produce corrections; geometry and confidence policies generate cues; and transparent rules adapt later practices. Video is never stored or uploaded. Judges can exercise the same analysis path through a reliable camera-free landmark demo.

The current build includes a 19-minute beginner routine, 12 poses and modifications, confidence-aware timing, immediate controls, local derived history, deletion, progress, personalization, and tests. Live model inference remains clearly marked as Phase 2 until a MoveNet ONNX artifact is fully verified. Saha is fitness education, not medical care, and avoids guarantees or body scoring.

The impact is a calmer, more responsive home practice without requiring an account or surrendering video to the cloud. The next milestone is validated local MoveNet inference across diverse users and environments.
